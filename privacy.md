# Privacy Policy

Canvas Rclone is a personal application used only by the owner of this Google account.

The application accesses Google Drive solely to upload, synchronize, organize, and maintain the user's own university course materials.

No personal information or Google Drive data is sold, shared, transferred, or disclosed to third parties.

The application does not use Google Drive data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

All data remains under the control of the Google account owner.
